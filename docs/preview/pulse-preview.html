Pulse, running on demo data

A clickable look-alike of the iOS app's screens. Every number is computed by the real PulseCore code (metrics engine v, recovery breakdown, sleep timeline, insights) from a simulated month of heartbeats. The layout copies the SwiftUI screens; the real app's screenshots come from the macOS build.

Try it

  • Tap the recovery ring on Today to see what moved the score.
  • Tap the tabs: Sleep has the night timeline, Activity has heart-rate zones, Trends has insights.
  • On Trends, switch between 7 and 30 days.

What's real and what isn't

  • Real: every metric, explanation, factor point, target, timeline segment and insight. They come from MetricsEngine, RecoveryExplainer, SleepTimeline and TrendInsights, the same functions the app calls.
  • Simulated: the heartbeats and movement. The band hasn't arrived yet.
  • Approximated: the visuals. Fonts, spacing and chart styling are close to the SwiftUI code, not pixel-identical. Settings and the welcome screens aren't recreated here.
