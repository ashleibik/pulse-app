Pulse, running on demo data

A clickable look-alike of the iOS app in its monochrome design. Every number is computed by the real PulseCore code (metrics engine v, recovery breakdown, sleep timeline, insights) from a simulated month of heartbeats. The real app's screenshots come from the macOS build.

Try it

  • Switch the phone between Light and Dark above it.
  • Tap See why on Today to see what moved the score.
  • Tap the tabs: Sleep has the night timeline, Activity has heart-rate zones, Trends has insights.
  • On Trends, switch between 7 and 30 days.

How the design works

  • No color. Recovery is the ring's fill plus one word: Ready, Steady or Rest.
  • One answer per screen. Each screen opens with one big number; details sit behind a tap.
  • Charts: past days light gray, today black, no gridlines.

What's real and what isn't

  • Real: every metric, sentence, factor point, target, timeline segment and insight. They come from MetricsEngine, RecoveryExplainer, SleepTimeline and TrendInsights, the same functions the app calls.
  • Simulated: the heartbeats and movement. The band hasn't arrived yet.
  • Approximated: the visuals. Fonts, icons and spacing are close to the SwiftUI code, not pixel-identical. Settings, the welcome pages, workout details and the metric sheets aren't recreated here.
